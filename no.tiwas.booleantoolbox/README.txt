Smart (Components) Toolkit adds the building blocks that ordinary Flows lack. Turn several sensors into one simple answer, such as "a window is open while nobody is home", and use it everywhere as a single device. Combine readings from many sensors into one value, remember how your lights were set and bring them back after the doorbell has rung, or let your lights follow the day: bright and cool in the daytime, warm and dim in the evening.

Your Flows become fewer, shorter and easier to understand. Handy cards let a Flow wait for a device, react only when everyone has really been away for a while, and compare or convert numbers without extra variables. Step-by-step guides, examples and online tools are available in the app's documentation.
